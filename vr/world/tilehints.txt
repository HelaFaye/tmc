== does the light agree with north-west? ==
  The statistic is max(0, cos) between a tile's bright-vs-
  dark axis and the north-west light. Averaged over RANDOM
  directions that is 1/pi = 0.318, so 0.318 means 'no
  information', not 'lit from the south-east'. Most tiles
  are texture and have no meaningful normal, so the
  all-tile figure sits near the null by construction. The
  number that matters is the one for tiles that are
  actually objects -- outlined, and shaded in real faces.

  null (random normals)                     0.318
  all tiles with >1 shade    n= 30809      0.285
  outlined, 3+ shades        n=  6349      0.331

  Measured separately on known objects, which is the
  cleanest test available (see tools/viewangle.py):
    chests   n=306   0.640
    torches  n=223   0.557
  Both about double the null, from two unrelated object
  classes. The north-west light stands.
